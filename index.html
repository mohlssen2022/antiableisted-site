Flagship service

Systems Audit and Equity Roadmap

We look across your system — policy, structures, staffing, and practice — to see how it serves students with disabilities today, and where it falls short.

Talk about an audit

The roadmap drives a right-sized implementation plan — built for your team's actual capacity, not an ideal one.

Services

Each engagement is scoped to your system — a single session, a series, or ongoing support, delivered in person, virtually, or hybrid.

Strategic Planning
Set direction for inclusive education across your system, grounded in your data and your community.
Systems and Program Design
Design or rework the structures, programs, and policies that shape how students with disabilities are served.
Professional Development
Professional learning for leaders and educators that is universally designed, relevant, and ready to use.
Coaching
Ongoing support for leaders and teams as they put new practices in place.
Implementation Support
Hands-on help turning plans into day-to-day practice, with continuous improvement built in.

Who we serve

School systems

Districts, charter networks, and schools. We connect systems design at the region and CMO level with the school-level structures and practices teams need to make real-time shifts.

Funders and nonprofits

We advise education funders and nonprofits on disability-inclusive philanthropy.

Areas of expertise

Instruction and learning

  • Universal Design for Learning (UDL)
  • Specially designed instruction and differentiation
  • Co-teaching, related services, and paraprofessional collaboration

MTSS, behavior, and well-being

  • Multi-tiered systems of support (MTSS)
  • Proactive behavior supports and de-escalation
  • Social-emotional learning, trauma, and mental health supports

Compliance, policy, and funding

  • Special education compliance
  • School-wide systems and policies
  • Funding and policy

Leadership and equity

  • Inclusive and adaptive leadership
  • Anti-ableism, accessibility, intersectionality, and disproportionality
  • Continuous improvement and implementation science
